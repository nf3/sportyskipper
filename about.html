About Us

We built the tool we wished we'd had on the sideline.

SportyClipper exists so coaches, scouts, parents, and athletes can spend their time on the game, not on a video timeline.

Our story

SportyClipper started on the sidelines. We spent too many nights scrubbing through game film to find a handful of plays, and we knew the hours were better spent coaching, recruiting, and watching the game.

So we built AI that clips the film for you, whether you need it live or after the final whistle. Today we work with teams from youth leagues to college programs, and our goal hasn't changed: get every team the film that matters, fast.
